## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Salvation: Echoes of War**. This trainer provides quality-of-life options for exploring the game's nightmare environments, avoiding hostile souls, and working with the dog-like companion. It focuses on stealth, enemy awareness, movement, and survival without turning the game's non-aggressive combat into a conventional weapon-based system.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed way to experience the story, explore the purgatory environments, solve puzzles, and study enemy behavior without repeated stealth failures.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Launch the game and load a playable session before activating movement options. Use the speed control for repositioning or exploration, then return to the default value before scripted scenes, transitions, or level changes.

### Tools & Inventory

The game centers on exploration, puzzle-solving, stealth, and companion interaction rather than a traditional equipment-heavy inventory system. The trainer therefore does not provide conventional item duplication or ammunition functions.

### World & Environment

Explore the surreal purgatory carefully and use the trainer to reduce interruptions while examining environmental details, locating secrets, and completing navigation puzzles. Enemy awareness controls are most useful in areas where light and sound affect detection.

### Combat & Weapons

Salvation: Echoes of War uses strategic, non-aggressive survival instead of direct enemy defeat. Activate No Detection or Enemy Awareness Reset when hostile souls begin a pursuit, and rely on evasion, positioning, and the companion's support to progress.
